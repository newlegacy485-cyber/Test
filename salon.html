Our Story

More than a salon.
A sanctuary.

Founded in 2014 by master stylist Isabelle Moreau, Maison Éclat was born from a simple belief: every person deserves to feel genuinely beautiful. We combine Parisian artistry with California ease to create looks that feel effortlessly, authentically you.

Our 3,000 sq ft studio is a calm oasis in the heart of San Francisco — stocked exclusively with clean, sustainable beauty products and staffed by stylists who are as passionate about their craft as they are about their clients.
