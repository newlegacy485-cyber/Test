# Parksart Customs Design Inspiration

This website should feel like a cinematic commission journey for a premium custom sneaker artist: dark luxury, culture-led, emotionally specific, and built to convert rented Facebook attention into owned buyer demand.

## 1. Brand Positioning

- Core message: freehand sneakers for people who refuse to wear the same story twice.
- Premium positioning: one-of-one wearable art shaped by Parker Joseph Ellis' taste, hand, and cultural point of view.
- UVP: 100% freehand custom sneaker work from a trusted Greenville, NC solo artist with a large organic audience.
- Transformation: blank mass-produced pair and scattered DMs become a personal artifact, qualified commission request, and memorable reveal.
- Voice: confident, artistic, Carolina-rooted, premium, culture-literate, direct.

## 2. Website Story Arc

- Hook: immediate hero with emotional headline, sneaker visual, and proof.
- Problem: audience exists, but DMs leak demand and hide pricing/context.
- Trust: 92K+ organic followers, freehand process, clear commission lanes.
- Transformation: before-and-after buyer journey from blank shoe to one-of-one story.
- Process: apply, concept, paint, deliver.
- Proof: quotes, market context, portfolio transformation cards.
- Offer: commissions, collector waitlist, drops, events, and B2B activations.
- CTA: repeated strategic waitlist and commission request paths.

## 3. Full Website Section Plan

- Cinematic hero with strong headline hierarchy and custom sneaker art.
- Brand positioning and founder-led story.
- Emotional problem and opportunity statement.
- Bento-style commission and service offer cards.
- Portfolio and transformation showcase.
- Limited drop concept for urgency.
- Visual process timeline.
- Trust/proof quote slider.
- Strategic FAQ.
- Final cinematic CTA and waitlist application form.

## 4. UI/UX Design Direction

- Mobile-first responsive layout.
- Premium whitespace, large editorial type, and strong contrast.
- Glassmorphic cards with depth, glow, and subtle grain.
- Bento grids for brand positioning, services, proof, and FAQ.
- Scroll-based reveal storytelling and sticky/floating CTA.
- Smooth micro-interactions, hover states, validation feedback, and accessible form states.
- Conversion-focused flow from attention to email capture.

## 5. Visual Style

- Mood: dark luxury, Apple Keynote polish, sci-fi/AI startup atmosphere, sneaker-culture warmth.
- Palette: deep blacks, warm gold, coral, violet, aqua, and refined gradients.
- Type: modern sans for UI, elegant serif for cinematic headlines.
- Effects: atmospheric lighting, soft glows, glass cards, subtle texture, and editorial spacing.

## 6. Future Tech Stack Direction

If this static prototype becomes a production platform, migrate to:

- Next.js 15+ App Router.
- React and TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS.
- Framer Motion plus GSAP for advanced scroll sequences.
- shadcn/ui components.
- Lenis smooth scroll.
- Vercel deployment.
- SEO metadata, Open Graph, schema, optimized images, lazy loading, and performance budgets.
