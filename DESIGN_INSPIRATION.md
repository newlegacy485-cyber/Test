# Roman's Cleaning Service Design Inspiration

This website should feel fresh, bright, trustworthy, and easy to act on for a local Easton, PA cleaning service.

## 1. Brand Positioning

- Core message: spotless homes and businesses with simple scheduling and reliable local care.
- Proof points: 4.8 Google rating, 23 reviews, women-owned business, responsive walkthrough setup, and residential plus commercial service.
- Voice: clear, warm, professional, direct, and neighborly.

## 2. Website Story Arc

- Hook: clean hero with immediate service promise, phone CTA, Google profile CTA, and trust metrics.
- Trust: location, phone number, women-owned identity, reviews, and service-area details.
- Services: residential refresh, initial deep clean, and business cleaning.
- Interaction: quick estimate planner, review slider, animated counters, responsive navigation, and quote-request validation.
- CTA: repeated quote and phone paths that make booking friction low.

## 3. UI/UX Design Direction

- Bright, modern, mobile-friendly layout with strong whitespace.
- Soft glass cards, mint/teal/sky gradients, and subtle cleaning-inspired illustration.
- Clear service cards with practical bullets and obvious next steps.
- Accessible form states, focus styles, hover effects, and status messaging.

## 4. Visual Style

- Mood: airy, polished, dependable, and local.
- Palette: clean white, soft mint, teal, sky blue, warm gold accents, and deep ink text.
- Type: modern sans for UI with editorial serif headlines for a premium feel.
- Effects: subtle grain, gentle glow, floating room illustration, animated sparkle details, and reveal-on-scroll motion.
